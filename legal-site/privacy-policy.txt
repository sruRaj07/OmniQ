OMNIQ - PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 20 August 2026
Applies to the OmniQ Android app (com.sruraj.omniq)

OmniQ is an online marketplace in India where buyers order goods from independent sellers. This
policy explains exactly what personal data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how
you can get it deleted. It is written to describe what the software actually does.

THE SHORT VERSION

We collect the details needed to create your account and deliver your orders: your name, email
address, phone number and delivery address. We do NOT collect your location, we do NOT use your
camera, we do NOT run any advertising, analytics or tracking software, and we do NOT sell your
data to anyone. Orders are Cash on Delivery, so we never handle your card or bank details. You can
delete your account from inside the app at any time and it happens immediately.


1. WHO IS RESPONSIBLE FOR YOUR DATA

OmniQ is operated by Sru Raj Sinha, a sole proprietor trading as "OmniQ", based in India. For
anything in this policy, including privacy requests, contact:

Email: srusinha092@gmail.com

We aim to respond to privacy requests within 30 days.


2. WHAT WE COLLECT

Information you give us:

- Email address and password. Collected when you create an account. Used to identify you and sign
  you in. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider; we never see or store them in
  readable form.

- Full name. Collected at sign-up and in your profile. Used so sellers know who to deliver to, and
  to address you in the app.

- Phone number. Collected in your profile and at checkout. Used so the seller or delivery agent can
  contact you about your order.

- Delivery address and PIN code. Collected in your profile and at checkout. Used to deliver your
  order and to check whether we serve your area.

- Photos you choose. Collected only if you are a seller listing a product, or an administrator
  uploading a banner. Used to display the product image. We only ever open your photo library when
  you tap to add an image, and we only receive the specific images you pick. Buyers are never asked
  for photos.

- Seller business details. Collected if you register as a seller. Used to operate your storefront
  and route orders and payouts to you.

Information created as you use the app:

- Cart contents, so your cart survives closing the app and switching devices.

- Order history - items, quantities, prices, delivery address, status and timestamps - to process
  and track orders, handle disputes, and meet tax and accounting obligations.

- Your account role (buyer, seller or administrator), to decide which parts of the app you can use.


3. WHAT WE DELIBERATELY DO NOT COLLECT

This section is here because it is unusual, and because you should be able to hold us to it. The
OmniQ app does NOT collect any of the following:

- Location. Not precise, not approximate. The app requests no location permission at all, and the
  permission is explicitly stripped from the app package. Serviceability is checked from the PIN
  code you type in, not from your device.

- Camera, microphone or audio. These permissions are explicitly stripped from the app package. The
  app can open your photo library on request, but it cannot take a photo or record you.

- Payment or financial information. All orders are Cash on Delivery. There is no payment gateway in
  the app, so no card, UPI or bank details are ever entered or stored.

- Advertising, analytics or tracking. There is no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no
  crash-reporting SDK and no advertising identifier. We do not build advertising profiles and we do
  not track you across other apps or websites.

- Contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs or installed apps.

- Push notification tokens. The app sends no push notifications.


4. HOW YOUR DATA IS USED

We use your data only to:

- create and secure your account, and sign you in;
- show you products and keep your cart;
- place, deliver, track and support your orders;
- let sellers fulfil the orders you place with them;
- prevent fraud and abuse, and keep the marketplace safe;
- meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making that produces legal effects, and we do not
sell or rent it.


5. WHO YOUR DATA IS SHARED WITH

Sellers. OmniQ is a marketplace, so this one matters. When you place an order, the seller you
ordered from receives your NAME, PHONE NUMBER and DELIVERY ADDRESS for that order. They need it to
deliver. Sellers only ever see the orders placed with them, and they never see your password or
your orders with other sellers.

Service providers:

- Supabase - database and authentication. Holds your account, profile, cart and orders. Acts as our
  data processor.

- Microsoft Azure - application hosting. Processes requests in transit. Our backend services run
  here.

- Google Play - app distribution. Handles installation and updates under Google's own privacy
  policy. We receive no personal data from Play.

Legal disclosure. We may disclose data where we are legally required to, such as a valid order from
a court or law enforcement agency, or where it is necessary to establish or defend a legal claim.


6. WHERE YOUR DATA IS STORED

Our infrastructure runs on servers operated by the providers above, which may be located outside
India, including in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. By using OmniQ
you consent to this transfer.


7. HOW YOUR DATA IS PROTECTED

- All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS.
- Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and are never stored in readable form.
- Database row-level security means one account cannot read another account's rows, even if the app
  were tampered with.
- Session tokens are cryptographically signed and verified on every request.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your
personal data we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.


8. HOW LONG WE KEEP YOUR DATA

- Account and profile data - until you delete your account.

- Cart contents - until you clear the cart or delete your account.

- Order records - retained after account deletion in anonymised form, with your name, phone number
  and delivery address stripped out and the link to your account removed. Indian tax and accounting
  law requires us to keep records of transactions; keeping them without identifying details is how
  we meet that duty without keeping data about you.


9. DELETING YOUR ACCOUNT

In the app: Profile > Data & Privacy > Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent - there
is no waiting period and no review step.

Without the app: email srusinha092@gmail.com from the address registered to the account, with the
subject "OmniQ account deletion request".

When you delete your account we immediately:

- erase your name, email address, phone number, saved addresses and PIN code;
- delete your sign-in credentials, so the account can no longer be used;
- empty and delete your cart;
- remove your name and address from past orders and unlink them from you;
- unlink any seller storefront from you as a person.

This cannot be undone. Your account cannot be recovered afterwards.


10. YOUR RIGHTS

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other applicable law, you have the
right to:

- ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
- have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected - you can edit most of it in the app directly;
- have your data erased, which you can do yourself at any time (section 9);
- withdraw consent, by deleting your account;
- nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become
  incapacitated;
- complain to the Data Protection Board of India if you believe we have mishandled your data.

To exercise any of these, email srusinha092@gmail.com.


11. CHILDREN

OmniQ is not intended for children. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account, since
ordering goods forms a contract. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a
child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.


12. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. If the change materially
affects how we handle your data, we will give notice in the app before it takes effect. Continuing
to use OmniQ after a change means you accept the updated policy.
