About OmniQ

Your local marketplace, delivered fresh

Our Story

OmniQ was born from a simple idea: bring the trust and freshness of your neighbourhood market into a modern app experience. We saw how local sellers — vegetable vendors, pooja shops, grocery stores — serve their communities with care but lack the digital tools to reach more customers.

We built OmniQ to bridge that gap. A multi-vendor marketplace where buyers can discover daily deals on fresh produce, pooja essentials, and everyday groceries — and where sellers can grow their business without any upfront costs.

Today, OmniQ connects hundreds of buyers with trusted local sellers across India, with a focus on fair pricing, transparent delivery fees, and absolute respect for your privacy.

What We Believe In

Every decision we make is guided by these principles

🛡️

Privacy First

No tracking, no ads, no data selling. We strip location and camera permissions from the app entirely. Your data is yours.

🤝

Fair for Everyone

Transparent pricing with daily offer limits. Buyers get great deals and sellers get fair margins. Win-win.

🌿

Fresh & Local

We prioritise local sellers who know their produce. Shorter supply chains mean fresher goods on your table.

⚡

Simple & Fast

No clutter, no unnecessary features. Browse, add to cart, checkout. Cash on delivery. That's it.

🏪

Empower Sellers

Zero listing fees, easy product management, and direct order notifications. We grow when our sellers grow.

💰

Honest Pricing

Delivery fees are calculated transparently. Orders ≥₹200 get free delivery. No hidden charges, ever.

How OmniQ Works

Three roles, one seamless platform

For Buyers

Browse categories, enjoy daily offer prices on fresh vegetables, add to cart with smart tiered pricing, and pay cash on delivery.

For Sellers

List products with images, set offer prices and limits, manage your storefront, and receive orders with buyer details for fulfilment.

For Admins

Manage categories, approve sellers, configure delivery zones, oversee the marketplace, and maintain product quality.
